论文名称：Actions as Language: Fine-Tuning VLMs into VLAs Without Catastrophic Forgetting
	
arxiv：2509.22195
	
概要：Instead of directly fine-tuning VLMs with discrete action tokens to become VLAs, which results in catastrophic forgetting, they relabel robot datasets with subtasks, actions as text and intermediate motion-planning like "move left". This training method is able to bridge the domain gap of VLMs without reducing performance on other VQA benchmarks from pretraining. Finally cheap LORA finetuning is enough to get strong results for action prediction while maintaining VLM reasoning capabilities.
